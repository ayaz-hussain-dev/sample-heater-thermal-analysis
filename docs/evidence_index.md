# Evidence index

The original images are retained under evidence/originals. Display copies are cropped only. Exact crop boxes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in evidence/crop_manifest.json; contours, labels, values and application branding are unchanged.

CATIA_A_Base_Shape.png shows the first-well sketch, and Material_Aluminium_Alloy_1.png shows the Workbench schematic. The descriptions below identify each image by its content. All numerical histories match their listed design and time step.

## Data provenance

All 18 original numerical files are in data/raw. Their raw hashes, row counts and clock checks are in results/data_audit.csv. The eight case definitions are in data/cases.json. The original archive supplied 63 files: 45 PNG images and 18 TXT histories.

## Display and interpretation

The ANSYS legends are left on their original independent scales. Similar colours between designs do not imply equal temperatures. Contour averaging settings and scope differ by quantity; the original Average column is retained without relabelling it as a physical volume average.
